### Changed — `--grep` leaves out the plain `<enc>` rows

`--grep` no longer prints an `<enc>` row that only repeats a name every hit's `in=` already carries, with its `callers=`/`defs=`/`cx=` (and the `callers_floor=`/`floor_next=` that qualify that count). The root names the rows it left out as `enc_cut=N`, defined in the answer's own legend, and the clauses that defined the dropped attributes leave the legend with them. Rows that say more stay: `--handles` (`h=`/`handle_omitted=`), a metrics lens (`amp=`/`tested=`) and an L4 carrier (`reads=`/`vals=`). Fewer than three plain rows beside a kept row are not cut, since `enc_cut=` and its clause would cost more than they save. The cut is a payload cut in both legend dialects, so the compact answer stays a legend-only change of the full one (the full prose defines `enc_cut=` when it rides). The grep render the `--for` route evidence reads keeps every row. `RIPWIRE_GREP_SLIM=0` restores the previous bytes. The MCP `grep` verb is unchanged.

Measured against main 03cbe7115 on 17 cells: the brief-shape-audit grep questions on jq, vscode, zed and cockroach (10; llvm left out, it runs on the M5) and the seven fire321 `--grep` calls (hono, textual, tmux). The median answer went from 4,524 B to 3,914 B, and the total from 100,353 B to 83,291 B (17 %). Every hit site was identical in all 17 cells, and the PREREG items stayed at 76 of 76. With `RIPWIRE_GREP_SLIM=0` the output was byte-identical to the base on all 17. `test/grepslimcheck.sh` holds the change. Its arms: the OFF answer carries plain rows and the ON answer carries none, with `enc_cut=` equal to that count (it fails if no case has rows to cut); OFF less those rows equals ON; ON is smaller; the legend swap; the tight-size rule; the full-legend payload equals the compact payload; `--handles` is identical ON and OFF. `docs/EVALS.md` §5 fact 1 is rewritten: the twelve uncapped small-hit queries of `test/grepbytescheck.sh` moved from a +61.5% payload increase over plain grep to parity (a 4.0% median cut on the dev machine, 4 of 12 larger), and that arm now holds a ±40-point parity band instead of a sign.
