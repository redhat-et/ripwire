### Fixed — `--for --token-budget`: `past_target=` and `files_first=` count the rows the answer still shows

Under an explicit `--for --token-budget=N` the answer is the unbudgeted answer shed to the budget, and its `rows:N` unit cuts the worst-ranked rows first. The shed rewrote `<sigs past_target="K">` as the unbudgeted K less the rows it cut, which is true only when the rows past the target are the last ones; the head round-robin (it lifts other files' best rows into ranks 1..8) and the docs reorder (moved doc rows take the worst ranks) both renumber them. It rewrote `files_first="N"` as at most the rows kept, which is true only when every kept row is one the round-robin moved. On the 89 GA1 DEV questions, `files_first=` claimed more moved rows than the answer showed on 83 of the 87 checkable answers at `--token-budget=1600` (for example 4 rows and `files_first="4"` where 3 had moved, or none), and `past_target=`'s legend reading stayed behind without its attribute on 74.

The shed now counts both over the rows it keeps, by the ranks the render gave those rows. A count of zero leaves the attribute and its legend reading out, as `docs_after_code=` already did. **Which rows are shown, their order and their text do not change**: the shed still prices its cut at the old counts, so it keeps exactly the rows it kept before. `est_tokens=` prices the document actually printed, so it changes by the bytes the recount adds or removes. The unbudgeted answer, `--json`, the MCP `for` verb and `--pack-task` have no shed after the render and are unchanged. Gate: `test/forbudgetcountscheck.sh`.
