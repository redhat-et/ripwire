### Added — `--for` prints the top-ranked doc sections for a prose question about the docs

A `--for` task that reads as prose, whose first ten file-tail rows (read in rank order) hold two or more doc files (`.md`, `.markdown`, `.mdx`, `.rst`, `.txt`, `.html`, `.htm`), now ends with `<doc_body>`: the sections of the first three doc files the answer shows that rank highest for the task's words, chosen the way `--recall` chooses them. Each section row names its file, source lines, heading and rank in that file.

"Reads as prose" is a cue list, not a parse: the task holds no backtick, `::`, `()`, lowercase snake_case word that ends in a letter or underscore, camelCase word, capital-lowercase-capital word, or dotted call `a.b(`. A task that is code-shaped in other ways still reads as prose and fires: `handle_upload_3`, `MAX_RETRIES`, `__init__`, `HTTPServer` and `getX` all do (`fordocbodycheck` pins it, and the block's legend says so).

The block has its own allowance of 7500 bytes of text, so the rest of the answer is unchanged, and it prices itself: `est_tokens=` on the `<doc_body>` root counts the block alone (the answer's `est_tokens` leaves it out). The files share the allowance by `--recall`'s water-filling; a section that does not fit its file's share is skipped and the next tried, and the unspent rest goes to the best section still left: a section too big for it is an excerpt around the densest window of the task's words (`cut="1" range=S-E/N`, bytes S to E of the section's N). A cut says `capped="1"`, `dropped=` and `next=` the `--recall` call; a file that served nothing is an `<f p= why=>` row with its reason (`unreadable`, `allowance` or `budget`).

A section is served only when it is about the task's subject: it must hold a third of the task's subject words, at most three (subject words are its distinct words of three or more letters that are not common English nor `doc`, `docs`, `describe`, `explain`, `show`), except that each file's top-ranked section is always kept (it can be worded around the mechanism while the task is worded around the symptom). Sections that hold fewer are counted in `below_floor=` on the root (`"below_floor"` in `--json`). `fordocbodycheck` arm q1 pins it.

A section is a lexical match, not a checked answer, and the block says so twice: `match="lexical"` on the root and a sentence in the legend (`--json`: `"match":"lexical"` and `"pick"`).

Under a token budget (`--token-budget`, `--max-tokens` with `--detail`, MCP `budget_tokens`) the answer is served first and the block takes the tokens left after it: the allowance shrinks until the priced block fits, and only what does not fit is held (`held="budget" held_bytes=` on the root, `<f p= why="budget" held=N>` per file, `next=` the same call without the budget). A budget the answer alone spends holds the block whole (the files named, with its own `est_tokens=`). `--json` carries the block as a `doc_body` key (`unserved` entries carry a `why`) and MCP `for` carries the same element over its own rows. `RIPWIRE_NO_FOR_DOC_BODY=1` turns it off. Gate: `test/fordocbodycheck.sh`.
