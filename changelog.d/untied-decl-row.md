### Fixed — an untied declaration is listed beside `defs=`, never silent

When a one-group `--callers`/`--uses`/`--impact` answer counted a same-named declaration in `defs=` that the identity rule could not tie to the definition (another function, or a tie it cannot prove, such as a template member's in-class declaration), the declaration was dropped from the `<def>` table while `defs=` still counted it. It is now listed after the group's rows as `<def ix=K … bodyless="1"/>` with `K` past the groups and no row attributed to it, so the rows listed equal `defs=`; a present-only legend clause says so.
