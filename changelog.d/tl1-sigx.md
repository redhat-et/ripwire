### Changed — page 1 of a `--for` answer carries the short form of the sigs rows past rank 10; their signature, doc and lens follow the rows

On the unbudgeted answer-first `--for` answer (the default, `--legend=last`, paged or `--page-bytes=0`), every `<sigs>` `<d>` row with `r` above 10 keeps `l= n= p= r=` and is self-closing; its other attributes and its text (the `<doc>` excerpt and the signature) are re-emitted verbatim as `<x r="R" …>text</x>` in one `<sigx>` block after the last row block, behind a `<!-- sigx: … -->` legend, and `est_tokens=` is re-priced over the added bytes. Nothing is dropped: putting every `<x>` back on its `<d r=R>` rebuilds the answer byte for byte. The median `<sigs>` is about 7.3 KB and ranks 11-40 about half of it, so a 6 KB / 12 KB page reaches ~19 more tail rows.

This is AF3's frozen C2(10), built as specified (lane TL1, `results/TL1_TAIL_1118/PREREG.md` M1). Dev (design 154 / held-out 49 / T5b 41 / T5c 34 cells, simulated and checked against the binary): complete within 6 KB 50→59 / 22→25 / 13→15 / 18→22 and within 12 KB 73→75 / 32→33 / 26→29 / 25→26; T5b −328 tok/question delivered at 12 KB pages. What it does NOT mean: it is not flip-free by construction — a gold item carried only by a moved attribute or signature leaves its page (one design cell, `mem=` on an r=25 row, leaves 12 KB) — and cross-gold on a 6 KB / 12 KB prefix rises because rows arrive earlier (an owner call). An agent reading only page 1 loses the signature, doc and lens of ranks 11-40 there. Not applied under `--legend=first`, a token budget, `--signatures-only`, MCP, or non-XML output. `RIPWIRE_NO_SIGX=1` turns it off. `test/forsigxcheck.sh` pins the rebuild (S1, with a negative control), the short rows, placement, re-pricing and the opt-outs; `foranswerfirstcheck` and `forpagescheck` (P6) pin the permutation with SIGX off.
