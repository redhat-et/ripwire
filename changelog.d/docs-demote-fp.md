### Fixed — `--recall` no longer hides hand-written reference pages as "generated"

`--recall` ranks a generated document (a command capture, a test golden, an API dump) below every other match. Its size-and-fences test (at least 5x the median document and at least 35 % of lines inside ``` fences) also caught long hand-written reference pages full of short examples, so a page could miss even when its own heading was the query. The test now also requires that the document has almost no prose of its own: under 15 % of its bytes on non-blank, non-heading lines outside fences. Over the in-sample documentation repos, every compiler golden and fence-dump fixture the old test caught stays demoted (their prose share is 0.00), and the 74 hand-written guides, reference pages and blog posts it caught (prose share 0.31 or more) are ranked by relevance again. The marker test (a document that says it is generated) is unchanged. The header now names the demoted documents too: `generated_demoted_paths="a,b"` lists up to five, best-first, even when they fall past `--top-k`, and `generated_demoted_named=K` appears when it lists fewer than the tally. Thanks to @muayyad-alsadi, whose #409 and #428 started this documentation track.
