### Changed — a `--for` tail row now also names up to two of its file's symbols that share the task's words (`tsym=`)

A tail row's `sym=` names its file's top three symbols by lens rank. When the symbol a question wants shares the task's words only through a stem (`restoration` and `restorer`), the lens never scores it, so the tail listed its file and named other symbols in it. Each code tail row that prints `sym=` can now carry `tsym=`, after `sym=`: up to two more of that file's symbols whose names share a task word (identifier subtokens, a light stem, and a stop list of question words such as "which", "function" and "file"), best match first, then the most central by PageRank. `sym=` is byte for byte what it was. Doc rows, the `p=`-only rows, the head and the `<syms>` block are unchanged: `<syms>` reads the answer as if `tsym=` were not there, and a name it prints is cut from `tsym=`. A task whose words no unprinted name shares leaves the answer byte for byte as before. A present-only legend clause defines `tsym=` in the full, compact and MCP dialects; the JSON tail carries `"tsym"` and `"tsym_why"`. `RIPWIRE_NO_TAIL_SYM_TERMS=1` restores the previous tail (a measurement and ablation switch; `test/tailsymtermscheck.sh` pins both).

This is option G2 of the depth lab, which found it while following up the 0.7.0 rehearsal's depth finding. Replacing names (G1) lost 12 credited names on 217 graded answers, so only the append ships. The lab measured +7/−1 items on the 217 terse answers and +8/−0 on the full questions, at about +1 KB median per terse answer and +3.5 KB on the full question. The lane's own numbers are in the PR.
