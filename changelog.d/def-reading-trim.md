### Changed — the `<def>` legend readings are shorter with the same meaning

The `<def>` legend readings on `--callers`, `--uses` and `--impact` (and their MCP twins) are shorter with the same meaning: 22 B less on every such answer, 53 B less on an MCP `find_referencing_symbols` answer. Each reading still says: `defs=` groups; a definition, then its tied declarations; the same `ix=`; `bodyless=1` means no body; a one-group answer names the ONE group measured, at file:line, and no row carries `of_def`; on `--uses` a use-site row names no group. Completeness unchanged on the 48-question held-out set (no cell lost or gained a group, cross-gold unchanged). This takes back about 19 % of the `--callers` legend bytes and 14 % of the `--uses` legend bytes the definition rows added in this release; the byte bar those rows failed (at most +2 %) still fails, at about +8.1 % on `--callers`. Lane S1 (`dce3f8a50`); `test/verbdefrowscheck.sh` arm (11) bounds each reading.
