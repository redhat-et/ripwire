### Changed — `--for` anchor choice: a test-only anchor among several no longer commits the route; a path word picks one namesake

- `--for` and the MCP `for` verb: a name-exact anchor that has two or more definitions, none of them source (test, doc, data, CI or vendored copies), no longer commits the route to whichever copy was found first. The words rank instead (`route="subtoken+body…; anchor has no source definition (…), not committed: ranked as words; hint anchors: …"`), the anchors stay in `route=` as a hint, and confidence reads low as before. A name with ONE such definition (a test helper that is the only thing by that name, however often the task repeats it) or with none (an env var served by the literal block) keeps its name-exact route.
- The query-mention anchor: an identifier the task names with several definitions (`syncProxyRules` in iptables/, ipvs/, nftables/, winkernel/) is lifted at the one whose file stem or parent directory another task word names (`kube-proxy nftables syncProxyRules`). A word that names several of them, two words that name two different ones, or a word that names only a C-family prototype while the definition sits elsewhere choose none, and the ranking is as before. A dot that ends a sentence (`… in nftables.`) does not make the word a qualifier.
