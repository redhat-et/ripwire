### Changed — every co-change partner list says its support floor, and every `--situ` partner row its shared-commit count

`--situ`'s `[3]` section printed each partner as a percentage alone ("co-edited in 67% of commits"), which cannot tell 2 of 3 commits from 200 of 300. No co-change answer named the floor a partner had to reach, which is the reason a partner with fewer shared commits is missing. The co-change backtest (`bench/cochange`) found that this floor decides most of what a one-file question can answer. Every `[3]` row now ends `, together=N`, and the MCP `situational_awareness` `forgotten` rows carry `"together"`. The floor is stated wherever partners are listed: `floor="3"` on `--situ`'s `[3]` header, on `<cochange>` for `--cochange=FILE`, and on `--pack-task`'s `<partners>`. The JSON twins carry it as `cochange_floor`, `floor` and `partners_floor`, and both legends define it. No row moves: the lists are byte-identical apart from these attributes. New gate `test/cochangefloorcheck.sh`.
