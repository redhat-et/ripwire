### Fixed — a call a rule proved is no longer listed as a call site of a same-named method

A caller row's `sites_at=` (the callers verb, `--safe-delete`, the `--for` callers hop and its site pick, `--edit-check`'s sites) matched call sites by the callee's leaf name, so a caller with edges to two definitions of one name listed every call of that name under both rows. envoy's `FilterManagerImpl::onConnectionClose` showed its proven `close_action.closeSocket()` lines (145, 154) under the by-name `ConnectionImpl::closeSocket` and `RawBufferSocket::closeSocket` rows, which read as a proven site keeping by-name namesake edges; only line 153 (`latched_close_action_->closeSocket()`, through a `std::optional`) is matched by name. A call a rule proved now counts as a site of its own targets only, and a file- or scope-qualified `--uses` drops it the same way. A call matched by name keeps the name test, and a caller with no proven call of the name keeps its row and sites. The `sites_at=` legend reading says so in the same number of bytes.
