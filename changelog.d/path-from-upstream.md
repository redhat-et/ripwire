### Changed — `--for=path from A to B` keeps the path's own rows in the ranked head

In a task that asks `path from A to B` (opening with `path from`, or `trace`, `show`, `explain` or `what is` then `the call path from`, `the code path from` or `the execution path from`, and not also asking `who calls` or `callers of`), a ranked caller of A (a resolved call, not a by-name one) that A does not itself reach through resolved calls is upstream of the asked path. It is scored below every other row, keeping its own order; files_first may still list a file's best row early. The header says how many moved (`[path from: N callers of the from endpoint scored below the path's own rows]`); pack-task prints it as its own comment, so the compact legend keeps it. A caller of A that A reaches at any depth stays (recursion, an eval loop), and so does B itself. A's reach walk stops at 4096 symbols, and then nothing moves (`path_from_capped=1`). B need not name a symbol. Before, a newly bound caller of A (`python -m django`'s `management.execute_from_command_line()`) could push a hop under A past the 40-row head. The MCP `for` and pack-task twins apply the same rule.
