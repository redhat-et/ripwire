### Fixed — a Rust type or function reached through a `pub use` re-export now proves the call

Two shapes left a Rust call unbound (`declined_calls=`) although the code names its target exactly. First, a type that a module supplies through a glob re-export (`pub use engine_state::*;` in `engine/mod.rs`) named no class, so a parameter written `engine_state: &EngineState` after `use nu_protocol::engine::EngineState;` typed nothing and `engine_state.get_decl(id)` stayed open between every `get_decl` in the tree. The module walk now follows the globs at a module's level: one class is named when every glob that can supply the name agrees on it. Two same-named types behind two globs still name nothing, and neither does an in-tree glob beside a glob the walk cannot place (an outside crate may supply the name unseen). Second, a bare call whose name a `use` binds (`use nu_engine::redirect_env;` over `pub use eval::{redirect_env};` in `lib.rs`) was declined when a second free function of that name sat elsewhere in the same crate. Such a call now binds the one free function its `use` reaches through the re-exports, and a `use` that leads outside the tree, nowhere or two ways decides nothing, as before.

Measured on nushell (main `5139f7840` against this change, `--pin-census` dispositions over all 153 556 calls): bound 50 116 → 53 459, declined 47 545 → 44 211. `--callers` of `EngineState::get_decl` lists 53 callers with 20 declined sites (was 5 and 116), of `nu_engine::eval_block` 12 with 8 (was 2 and 19; the 8 are calls through a local closure of that name), of `redirect_env` 10 with 0 (was 3 and 7). On axum 21 calls moved from declined to bound and `TestClient::get` reads 119 proven callers where all 174 were `via="name"`. 20 changed edges sampled across both repositories were opened at the call site: 20 correct. 133 edges on nushell changed target; the ones opened had pointed at a same-named method or a private namesake before. What it does NOT mean: a glob of an enum's variants or of an outside crate is not followed, a `use … as` alias of a function is not read, and a call qualified by a crate path (`nu_engine::eval_block(..)`) resolves as before.
