### Fixed
- `--for`: a symbol named exactly in a descriptive subject is the anchor. Plain words beside it (`new`, `parameter`, `default`) no longer anchor the wrong definitions, decline the route or lose `<callers>`; `route=` lists them under `dropped:`. A conceptual route whose subject names one function carries `<callers by="subject">`.
- `--for`: `confidence="high"` on a name-exact route needs a source definition behind the anchor. An anchor only a test, doc or data file defines reads low, and `route=` says why.
