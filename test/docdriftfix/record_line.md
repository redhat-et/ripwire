# docdriftfix — an undated note whose individual LINES are dated

Neither the filename, the title nor the front matter dates this document, so `rec="title"` and `rec="stamp"`
must both stay silent here. Each anchor below dates ITSELF, which is the strongest evidence the lane has and
therefore the one it reports: `rec="line"`.

- RECORD (line, hedge) — the helper ended at code.h:902 at the time of writing.
- RECORD (line, as-of) — the helper ended at code.h:903 as of 2026-01-15.
- RECORD (line, log row) — a ledger entry stamped with its own day:

| 2026-01-15 | measured | the tail sat at code.h:904 |

- LIVE — and one plain sentence with no dating mark at all: the helper ends at code.h:905.
